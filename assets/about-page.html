THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROMPT

Hi, I’m Kaniz Fatiha.

I’m a WordPress developer from Bangladesh. My work also includes frontend development, quality assurance, and user support—so I care about both how a site is built and how it feels to use.

kanizfatiha.sh is my personal Linux journal. I share clear, practical notes for anyone curious about Linux, open-source software, and everyday computing.

What you’ll find here

  • Beginner-friendly Linux guides and terminal basics.
  • Thoughtful comparisons of desktop distributions and apps.
  • Practical notes on backups, privacy, gaming, and troubleshooting.

My approach

I aim to explain the “why” as well as the steps, link to useful sources, and call out compatibility or safety caveats. You don’t need to be an expert to get started; curiosity is enough.
